- Application Protocol -

PRECOM <SYN/ABRT>
- The master will begin by sending a PRECOM packet to all of its peers and wait for a response.
- Each peer will then reply with an ACK message if they are available.
- If the master does not receive an ACK from a peer in over 5 seconds, it will broadcast an ABRT message to all the peers, abort its local operation,
    and send an ERROR message to the client. The peers will then abandon the rest of the process and resume to their normal behavior.
- If all peers respond with ACK messages, the commit phase begins.

COM <next message id> <WRITE/REPLACE> <message>
- The master broadcasts a commit message containing the operation to perform and message to all its peers.
- Each peer will then attempt to perform the operation on the bbfile. The peer will reply to the master with an ACK  if the operation was successful
    or a NACK if it could not perform the operation.
- The master will wait for all peers to reply with their operation status. If all peers sucessfully performed the operation, the master will then perform
    the operation locally and broadcast OK if it was successful.
- If theres a NAK from any peer or the master's local operation was unsuccessful, the master will broadcast NOK, causing all peers to roll back their change.
